# Heap Delivery

Order bulk goods and have them delivered as a heap on your own land. Mark a delivery zone, choose the product and the amount, and after the delivery time the heap is placed in the zone.

## Products
Lime, manure, straw, hay and grass.

## Getting started
1. On foot, press **Alt + L** to open the Heap Delivery menu.
2. Press **New zone**, look at the ground where the heap should go and left click to place the zone (right click cancels).
3. On the zone's row press **Order**, choose the product and the amount, and confirm.
4. After the delivery time the heap appears in the zone and you get a message.

## Delivery zones
- Zones are circles 4, 6 or 8 m wide and can only be placed on land your farm owns, at least 1 m away from your other zones. Each farm can have up to 50 zones.
- While placing, the circle turns green where it can go and red with the reason where it can't. The slope of the ground under it is shown, with a warning on steep ground.
- Placing stops if you get into a vehicle, pick up a tool or object, or open a menu.
- Zones are named after their field (Field 3, a second one Field 3a; land without a field uses its farmland number). **Rename** adds a short description, for example Field 3 - front entrance. Descriptions go through the game's profanity filter.
- **Edit** on a zone's row opens **Size**, **Rename** and **Remove**.
- The menu shows each zone's size and what is lying in it. A full zone is shown in red.
- **Hide markers** in the menu hides the zone circles and labels for you only. Your farm's zones also show as markers on the map.

## Ordering
- The order screen shows the product with its icon, an amount slider in 500 L steps, and a receipt with the product price, the delivery fee and the total.
- The slider offers the room that is left in the zone for that product.
- The price is the current in-game price of the product, including the economy difficulty, plus a delivery fee. The money is taken when you order.
- Each zone takes one delivery at a time. Order stays grey until it arrives, and the menu counts down the time left.

## Sizes and products
- How much a zone holds depends on its size and the product. Light, fluffy products such as straw, hay and grass need much more room per liter than lime or manure.
- Different products can't share a heap. A zone only takes the product already lying in it until that heap is used up.

## Slopes
- The game can only keep a heap on ground that isn't too steep, and each product piles a little differently.
- On a slope, whatever the zone can't hold is tipped right next to it, on your own land and away from your other zones, so you still get the full amount. The delivery message says how much went next to the zone.
- Only when all the ground around is very steep is the rest refunded.

## History and refunds
- **History** on a zone shows its last 50 deliveries: when, which product, how much arrived, the amount paid in red and any refund in green.
- Refunds include their share of the delivery fee. If you sell the land before a delivery arrives, the whole order is refunded.
- The last few liters of an order are topped up for free instead of being refunded, because the game places heaps in small whole pieces.

## Settings
Under **Settings > Game Settings > Heap Delivery**:
- **Delivery time**: how long after ordering a delivery arrives (10 seconds to 60 minutes, default 1 minute). Real time, paused with the game.
- **Delivery fee**: added to the product price, 0% to 25% (default 5%).

Both are saved with the savegame. In multiplayer only the host or an admin can change them.

## Multiplayer
Supported. Zones, orders, history and settings are synced to all players. Zones belong to a farm, and only that farm can order for them.

## Languages
English, German, French and Italian.

## Controls
| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Alt + L | Open the Heap Delivery menu (on foot) |
| Left click / gamepad A | Place the zone (while placing) |
| Right click / gamepad B | Cancel placing |

All keys can be changed under Settings > Controls.
